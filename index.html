Chocoflan

Creamy Flan, Moist Chocolate Cake, and Lovely Caramel Coating.

About Ingredients Instructions

What is Chocoflan?

Chocoflan is a delicious Mexican dessert that combines a creamy flan and chocolate cake into one beautiful and tasty dessert!

Why it's Special

Chocoflan is my family's favorite dessert and we make it for every celebration.

Ingredients

Flan Ingredients

  • 1 cup of sugar
  • 6 eggs & 1 can of sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 can of evaporated milk
  • 1 tablespoon of vanilla extract

Cake Ingredients

  • 1 box of chocolate cake mix
  • 1 chocolate pudding packet
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 1/2 cup of oil
  • 4 eggs
  • 1/2 cup of sour cream or mayo

Trust me, adding sour cream or mayo makes the chocolate cake more fudgy and rich :)

Instructions

  1. Prepare the caramel and coat your desired mold with the caramel.
  2. Prepare and mix your flan ingrediets
  3. Strain and pour the flan mixture into your mold
  4. Cover the mold with aluminum foil and place it in a water bath
  5. Place it in the oven and bake for 40 mins
  6. Prepare and mix your cake ingredients
  7. Once the flan is done baking, gently pour the cake mixture on top of the flan. Cover it again with aluminum foil and keep it in the water bath
  8. Bake for another 55 mins
  9. Once done, remove it from the water bath and keep the cake covered so the moisture stays inside
  10. Once it reaches an even temperature, place it in the refrigerator overnight or for at least 4-5 hours
  11. Unmold and decorate with strawberries
  12. Ready to eat!

Serving Tip

For the best outcome, let your chocoflan chill completely over night before unmolding. Decorate with fresh strawberries and serve!

Enjoy!
